## 1. Why This Is a Separate Document

The copyright licenses in this repository are Creative Commons licenses. CC 4.0
§ 2(a)(5)(B) provides that a licensee may not impose additional or different
terms that restrict exercise of the licensed rights, and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
§ 3(b)(3) applies the same rule to adapted material.

An automation prohibition written into those files would therefore be an
additional restriction. It would make the CC grant non-standard, and — for Tier 2
branches — it could not lawfully be passed downstream. It would lapse at the
first branch.

Trademark and name rights are different. CC 4.0 § 2(b)(2) expressly states that
**patent and trademark rights are not licensed** under a CC Public License. Those
rights remain with the author, are untouched by the CC grants, and are not
subject to the no-additional-restrictions rule.

So the conditions below attach to the **names**, not to the text. They survive
into branches, because they never depended on the copyright license.

> **Practical effect.** You may copy, teach, adapt, and redistribute exactly as
> the CC licenses allow, without agreeing to anything in this document. What you
> may not do without agreeing to it is **call the result JPP**.

## 3. Automation Condition

You may not describe any automated or AI system, or any component of one, as
**JPP**, **CST–JPP**, **JPP-compliant**, **JPP-based**, or **JPP-aligned**, and
you may not use any protected designation to identify such a system, unless:

1. you hold **explicit written license** from the author for that use; and
2. the system maintains **human-in-the-loop safeguards** under which a
   identified human being holds and exercises the judgment, and the system does
   not issue, score, rank, or execute a judgment without that person.

**Legibility is permitted; delegation is not.** Machine evaluation of the
cardinal-virtue matrix for diagnostic, analytic, or formation purposes is within
scope. Substituting machine output for the operator's act of judgment is not.

> **Note on Module 03.** Module 03 describes the cardinal-virtue matrix as
> "legible to machine evaluation — without ever judging the agent." That module
> is derived teaching material, not governing text, and it states the permission
> without stating the limit. The limit is stated here, and this document governs
> name use. Where the two differ, this document governs.

## 4. Branch Condition

You may call an adaptation **CST–JPP-X**, or otherwise identify it by a protected
designation, only if it:

1. is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 per [`LICENSE-BRANCHES`](LICENSE-BRANCHES);
2. attributes JPP 1.3b as parent, names the author, and states the parent
   SHA-256;
3. carries a **written statement of divergence** from JPP 1.3b in the material
   itself — what was added, removed, reordered, renamed, or reinterpreted; and
4. is not enclosed, paywalled as the primary means of access, or sublicensed on
   restrictive terms.

A branch that does not meet these conditions may still be built and distributed
under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. It simply may not carry the name.

## 5. Formation Condition

You may hold yourself out as **training or forming others in JPP** only on
satisfying the Operator Readiness Checklist in the README.

This is a condition on the name, not on teaching. Teaching *from* the trunk, and
teaching *about* it, are permitted to every licensee under
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE) without reference to this document. What requires readiness
is presenting oneself as forming JPP operators.

**Readiness is self-attested unless the author certifies otherwise.** An
attestation that proves false is a breach of this policy, not a defect in the
licensee's copyright license.

## 6. Redline Misuses

The following uses of the protected designations are outside permission:

- **No Act Vector without Orient & Align.** Presenting a truncated cycle as JPP.
- **No use of altitude to bypass accountable stakeholders.** "High altitude" is
  a classification of the moment, not a warrant to route around the people owed
  an account.
- **No reordering or renaming of the phases** in any work claiming to be JPP.
  The phases are Frame, Receive & Sense, Orient & Align, Act Vector, Iterate.
- **No JPP-compliant claims for AI-only or automated workflows** (see Section 3).
- **No training of others** unless operator readiness is met (see Section 5).

## 7. Stewardship

Proper use presumes formation in ethics, authority, and consequence.
Misapplication — as a control device, a speed hack, or a proxy for conscience —
is outside scope and contrary to design.

CST–JPP is a discipline for human judgment. It is **not** legal, medical, or
administrative advice. Users remain responsible for their own decisions and
outcomes.
