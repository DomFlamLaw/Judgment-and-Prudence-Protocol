# CST–JPP

**Catholic Social Teaching + The Judgment and Prudence Protocol**

A two-part instrument for judgment under pressure. CST supplies the perception —
what kind of moment this is. JPP supplies the steering — what to do inside it.

> **CST–JPP is a discipline for human judgment, not a substitute for it.**

---

## 1. The Problem: Drift, Confusion, Opacity

Modern life accelerates faster than inherited judgment systems can metabolize.
The symptoms are everywhere:

- **Drift** — losing orientation in the noise
- **Confusion** — collapsing under competing values and narratives
- **Opacity** — lacking a way to see what matters and what to do next

These are structural conditions, not personal failures. When the world becomes
chaotic, the operator needs better instruments — not more willpower or slogans.
What is missing is a shared grammar for making sense of moments and a disciplined
method for acting inside them.

The CST–JPP interface provides both.

---

## 2. CST 2026: An Operator's Manual for Moments

CST is not a library of principles. It is an operator's manual for moments — a
way of reading the world that preserves dignity, agency, and responsibility under
pressure. God is the initial starting point and the hoped-for end in beatitude.

CST 2026 reframes the tradition as a moment-diagnostic system built on four
perennial questions:

1. What human dignity is at stake
2. What solidarity obligations are activated
3. What structures of justice shape the field
4. What common good must be protected or restored

These are axes of perception. CST gives the operator **moral altitude** — clarity
about what kind of moment they are in.

### The Framing Question: Universal Destination of Goods

Over the four questions sits a prior one, which sets the horizon against which
they are asked — the **Universal Destination of Goods (UDG)**:

> Does the act exploit, exclude, and extract — or does it provide for goods to be
> spread in common? Is it Babel, or the walls of Jerusalem communally rebuilt?

UDG is not a fifth axis. It is the question that tells the operator what the
other four are *for*.

*Editorial note: UDG does not appear in the frozen trunk (JPP 1.3b). It is
carried here as README-level framing pending its treatment in a future trunk
revision. See §11.*

---

## 3. JPP: Steering in a Chaotic World

**The Judgment and Prudence Protocol (JPP)** is the steering mechanism — a kata
for disciplined action when the world is loud and ambiguous.

Where CST clarifies **what matters**, JPP clarifies **what to do**.

### 3a. What JPP Provides

- **Orientation** — naming the moment and its forces
- **Stability** — preventing drift and distortion
- **Pace** — matching tempo without being swallowed by it
- **Agency** — keeping the human operator at the moral center

### 3b. How JPP Runs — The Canonical Structure

The trunk specifies the mechanism in four parts. These names and this order are
canonical; §8 prohibits reordering or renaming them in any work claiming to be
JPP.

**I. The Judgment Gyroscope** — the stabilizing core. Maintains orientation under
stress by anchoring the practitioner in situational clarity, emotional
steadiness, altitude awareness, and non-reactivity. The Gyroscope creates the
internal stillness required for clean perception.

**II. The Ethical Engine** — five sequential moves, each building on the last:

| # | Phase | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Frame** | Establish situation, scope, and stakes. Define what is being judged and why it matters. |
| 2 | **Receive & Sense** | Take in signals — factual, emotional, contextual. Distinguish signal from noise without collapsing into either. |
| 3 | **Orient & Align** | The heart of the engine. Three mandatory sub-tests, then altitude classification. |
| 4 | **Act Vector** | The integrated propulsion mechanism. Fires only on alignment. |
| 5 | **Iterate** | Reassess. Recalibrate. Re-enter the cycle with improved fidelity. |

**Orient & Align** runs three mandatory sub-tests:

- Probe margins, gaps, and ethical edges
- Assess the cost of inactivity; run the horizontal virtue vector (prudence,
  justice, fortitude, temperance)
- Audit along the vertical virtue axis: faith → hope → love

If action is triggered, classify the **altitude**:

- **Low altitude** — tactical, immediate, concrete
- **High altitude** — systemic, structural, long-arc
- **Noise-based dampening** — when the correct move is restraint

**Act Vector** fires only when three conditions align:

1. **Withdrawal stabilizes** — the movement in the moment is to pause for purpose
2. **Trigger Vector provides direction** — the body's spike becomes information,
   not command
3. **Virtue Matrix selects** the proportionate, ethical, role-aligned move

When these converge, the Act Vector fires — not from panic, not from ego, not
from reflex, but from alignment. This is **calibrated decisive action**.

**III. The Thomistic Stabilizers with Taoist Timing** — the virtue architecture
that prevents drift (see §4).

**IV. The Integrated Model** — clarity, direction, ethical constraint, and timely
execution, producing proportionate action and clean timing.

### 3c. Wu Wei Timing

The **Wu Wei timing vector** governs when not acting is the proper course: act
from a place of centeredness, pause for purpose, inactive passion.

> Let the tea steep. Gather seeds. Scent the tea. Palm the seeds. Breathe.
> Ground.

### 3d. The Two-Part Instrument

- **CST:** See the moment
- **JPP:** Steer the moment

---

## 4. The Architecture: How the System Holds Under Load

### 4a. Con + Facere: The Shared Act of Making

*Con + facere* names the disciplined collaboration between human agents,
communities, and the systems yoked to them. It is the antidote to isolation and
reactive fragmentation.

### 4b. The Yoke and Harness

The yoke does not spin, decide, or overpower. It constrains tilt, prevents drift,
and keeps the operator centered. It is a stabilizer, not a source of agency.

### 4c. Horizontal Stabilizers: The Cardinal Virtues

Prudence, Justice, Fortitude, Temperance — the plane that prevents roll into
excess or collapse. Operational, worldly, stabilizing: these govern proportion,
discipline, and right-sized action.

### 4d. Vertical Stabilizers: Faith, Hope, Love

The keel and direction. They prevent yaw — twisting away from the true course.
Transcendent, orienting, elevating: these govern intention, orientation, and
moral altitude.

### 4e. The Moment of Witness

Where judgment becomes testimony. Where the architecture proves itself in lived
reality.

### Canonical Closure

CST–JPP restores clarity, agency, responsibility, and the ability to act without
distortion. It is a manual for operating inside moments — **not a system for
judging people.**

### Automation Prohibition

The Automation Prohibition lives in
[`NAME-AND-USE-POLICY.md`](NAME-AND-USE-POLICY.md) §3, **not** in the copyright
licenses. This is deliberate.

CC 4.0 § 2(a)(5)(B) and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 § 3(b)(3) bar a licensee from imposing
additional restrictions on licensed or adapted material. An automation clause
written into the CC grant would make that grant non-standard and could not be
propagated by a Tier 2 branch — it would lapse at the first branch. Names are
different: CC 4.0 § 2(b)(2) expressly withholds trademark rights from the grant,
so conditions attached to the names survive into branches.

In substance:

> You may not describe an automated or AI system as **JPP**, **CST–JPP**, or
> **JPP-compliant**, or identify one by any protected designation, without
> explicit written license and human-in-the-loop safeguards.
>
> **Legibility is permitted; delegation is not.** Machine evaluation of the
> cardinal-virtue matrix for diagnostic and formation purposes is in scope.
> Substituting machine output for the operator's act of judgment is not.

Module 03 states the legibility permission without stating the limit. It is
derived teaching material, not governing text. The limit is stated in the policy.

## 9. Operator Readiness Checklist

Required before holding yourself out as **training or forming others** in JPP.
It is not a condition on teaching from the trunk, which every Tier 1 licensee may
do. Readiness is self-attested unless the author certifies otherwise.

All four must be **yes**:

1. Completed **three live cycles** — adversarial, time-compressed, and
   multi-party — each with a debrief.
2. Can resolve a **virtue conflict** (e.g. Prudence against Justice) before
   acting.
3. Has demonstrated **Wu Wei timing** — disciplined non-action — at least once.
4. Files brief debriefs: what JPP **prevented**, what it **enabled**, and where
   it **strained**.

Agreement to the attribution and no-derivatives terms is a condition of the
license itself, not a readiness criterion, and is addressed in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
